Who We Are

Founded in 1996, Riverside Academy has been a cornerstone of Nursery to Senior Secondary excellence in our community. We combine rigorous academics with a commitment to developing the whole child—fostering critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, and compassion.

Our dedicated faculty, small class sizes, and personalized attention ensure every student receives the support and challenge they need to thrive. We prepare students not just for college, but for meaningful lives of impact and purpose.

Our Core Values

1

Academic Excellence

We maintain rigorous standards and encourage intellectual curiosity through challenging coursework and innovative teaching methods.

2

Character Development

Beyond the classroom, we nurture ethical leadership, integrity, and a commitment to serving others with honor and respect.

3

Inclusive Community

We celebrate diversity and foster belonging for every student, creating an environment where all voices are heard and valued.

Our Academic Programs

Nursery / Early Years

Ages 2–5

Early literacy, numeracy, and social skills taught through play, songs, and guided exploration.

Primary School

Primary 1–6

A strong foundation in English, Mathematics, Basic Science, and Social Studies, with an emphasis on reading fluency and curiosity.

Junior Secondary School

JSS 1–3

Broad coursework across sciences, languages, and vocational subjects, preparing students for the BECE.

Senior Secondary School

SSS 1–3

Specialised Science, Arts, and Commercial tracks with focused preparation for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB.

How to Apply

1

Schedule a Tour

Visit Riverside and experience our community firsthand. Tour the campus, meet faculty and current students, and see where learning comes alive.

2

Submit Application

Complete our online application with your student's information, academic history, and a personal statement about your educational goals.

3

Admissions Interview

Meet with our admissions team for a discussion about your academic interests, extracurricular activities, and why Riverside is the right fit.

4

Enrollment

Upon acceptance, finalize your enrollment, complete the welcome documentation, and get ready to join the Riverside Academy community.
